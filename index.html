Welcome!

I am a PhD Candidate in Economics at Yale University. I focus on topics in Household Finance, Public Economics and Applied Microeconomics. My recent work studies how households insure against economic risk and save for retirement.

I will be on the 2026-2027 Job Market.

Before starting my PhD, I graduated summa cum laude with a BA in Economics-Mathematics from Columbia University and worked as a Research Professional at Chicago Booth. I was born and raised in Peru.
